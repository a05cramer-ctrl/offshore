How it works

Open your account

Connect a wallet and sign one message. It is free and moves nothing. That signature becomes the key to your offshore account, and it never leaves your browser.

Take a coin offshore

Pick any coin and how much. One transaction moves it into the Privacy Rings pool on Solana. Your wallet's balance of that coin drops by exactly that amount.

Off the board

Trackers read wallet balances, so they show what is left there: 0 if you moved it all. Your offshore balance is encrypted on-chain.

Bring it back

One click, no wallet popup. Your offshore key proves and signs the trip, and the coin lands back in the same wallet.

What people can still see

  • The trip out and the trip back. Both are normal Solana transactions: the coin and the amount show in your wallet's history.
  • Not your offshore balance. Inside Privacy Rings the coin and the amount are encrypted. Only your key decrypts them.
  • Not in your wallet. Anything that reads token balances (trackers, holder lists, bubble maps) counts only what is still onshore.
  • This hides what you hold, not that you moved it.

What $OFFSHORE does

Every move burns it

  • Every trip out pays 0.5% of the bag's value in SOL to one public wallet.
  • Every hour, once 0.02 SOL of fees has built up, that wallet buys $OFFSHORE on the open market and burns it. It keeps 0.01 SOL for network fees and spends the rest.
  • Both transactions are public. The fee wallet is listed under Verify.

How it is built

  • No custody. Coins sit in the Privacy Rings program. OFFSHORE has no key to them and no contract of its own.
  • Your key is yours. It is derived in your browser from your wallet's signature over one fixed message. Same wallet, same key.
  • Open toolkit. Moves are built with Helius's @heliuslabs/zolana 0.8.0-alpha. Proofs for trips back are made in an attested enclave; the request is encrypted in your browser.
  • Early software. The toolkit is an alpha release. Start with an amount you are fine waiting on.
  • OFFSHORE is built on Solana Privacy Rings and is not affiliated with Helius.

Questions

Which coins can go offshore?

Any SPL or Token-2022 coin, which covers every pump.fun coin, plus SOL. No listing and no approval. The first person to move a coin pays about 0.0024 SOL once to open its vault.

Which wallets work?

Any Solana wallet that can sign a message and a normal transaction: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and others. The trip back is signed by your offshore key inside the page, so it works the same on all of them.

What if this site disappears?

Your coins are not here. They sit in the Privacy Rings program on Solana. Your key comes from your own wallet's signature over a fixed message, so any copy of this page, or any tool built on the same toolkit, can rebuild it and bring your coins back.

What if I lose my wallet?

Then you lose the offshore account too. The offshore key is made from that wallet's signature. Keep the wallet's seed phrase safe, exactly as you already do.

Can someone else open my account?

Only with your wallet's signature over the OFFSHORE message. Never sign that message on a site you do not trust.

Does a coin offshore still count as held?

On-chain it sits in the pool's vault, so holder lists and snapshots that read wallets do not count it for you. Bring it back before anything that needs it in your wallet, such as an airdrop snapshot.

How long does a trip take?

Out: one normal transaction. Back: your browser asks the prover for a proof, then sends one transaction. You stay on the page for both.
